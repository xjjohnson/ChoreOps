# Fork roadmap

Ideas parked for future updates of the fork. Not scheduled; each goes through
the usual preview and approval loop before it is built.

## Chores

- **Unassigned chores without a stand-in person.** Today a chore has to be
  assigned to someone to appear on the dashboards, so a "Pool" person exists
  only to hold chores in standby (handy for chores that are not for anyone yet,
  or for testing). Goal: let a chore exist unassigned and still show under
  Standby, without the placeholder person.
- **Chores that become due from a Home Assistant entity.** A chore turns due
  when an entity changes (for example a sensor reports the dishwasher finished,
  the trash bin is full, or a door has been open a while) instead of only on a
  schedule.
